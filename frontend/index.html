ABOUT PRAMA-DYNAMAGH

What PRAMA-Dynamagh does

PRAMA-Dynamagh converts mandates into evidence-bound machine decisions. It acquires intelligence through Telegraph, preserves provenance, evaluates structural admissibility through PRAMAgraph, and issues deterministic Decision Tickets that remain traceable to the evidence that produced them.

How it works

Mandate → Telegraph → Miner → Evidence → PRAMAgraph → Decision → Ticket
Authority → Acquisition → Provenance → Structural evaluation → Actionability

Before an autonomous action continues, PRAMA-Dynamagh tests whether the acquired evidence is structurally admissible for the decision being made.

What has been demonstrated

  • Telegraph/x402 acquisitions with real Miner responses.
  • Persisted provenance from acquisition through Evidence.
  • Deterministic structural evaluation and PERMIT / REVIEW / BLOCK decisions.
  • Verifiable Tickets with replayable lineage.
  • Budgeted autonomous execution with economic and longitudinal authority gates.

Epistemic discipline

PRAMA does not equate a response with knowledge. Acquisition establishes access; provenance establishes origin; Evidence establishes an attributable object; structural evaluation tests whether that object can support a decision.

A Decision Ticket records not merely an answer, but the chain by which that answer became actionable.

Provenance is not truth. Verification is not evaluation. A decision must remain reconstructible from its evidence.

Why it matters

Autonomous agents increasingly act on machine-generated intelligence. PRAMA-Dynamagh introduces a structural layer between acquisition and action: one that preserves provenance, evaluates viability, constrains autonomy and leaves a verifiable decision artifact.

Acquire → Trace → Evaluate → Decide → Verify

Built on Telegraph

Telegraph provides machine-native access to Miners and intelligence. PRAMA-Dynamagh receives and structures those outputs so an autonomous system can reason over evidence without confusing provider response, provenance, evaluation and authority.

Telegraph intelligence → PRAMAgraph evaluation → governed action
